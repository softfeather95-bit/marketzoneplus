# Supplementary exact-device photography and price sources

These five records supplement categories that the Amazon Berkeley Objects selection did not cover with genuine standalone devices. Product photographs are exact-model works from Wikimedia Commons, stored locally as optimized WebP files. No manufacturer, retailer, AI-generated, unrelated-model, or visually similar substitute imagery is used.

All browser-captured photographs had only the surrounding black browser background cropped away. Other photographs were resized only. All were converted to WebP. These modifications do not change the depicted product. Adapted CC BY-SA images remain under their original CC BY-SA 4.0 license; CC BY images remain under CC BY 4.0.
